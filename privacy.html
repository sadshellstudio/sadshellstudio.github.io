Privacy Policy — Long Sky Walk

Last updated: August 2026

What Data We Collect

  • Google Account Data — your name and email address. Used only for sign-in and to sync your data across devices.
  • Step Data — daily step count, distance traveled, full history. This is the core feature of the app.
  • Journey Progress — current map location, cycle number, total kilometers walked.
  • App Settings — language, step source, notification preferences.
  • Usage Data — anonymous statistics about how the app is used (app opens, screens viewed) and crash reports. Not linked to your name or email.

Health Connect

With your permission, the app reads your step count from Health Connect to display your daily progress. This data is used solely for the app's core step-counting feature, is never used for advertising or shared with third parties, and remains under your control. You can revoke access at any time in your device's Health Connect settings.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the app: displaying your progress, sending milestone notifications, and syncing between devices. We do not sell or share your data with any third parties.

Where Data Is Stored

  • Locally — on your device (Android SharedPreferences).
  • In the cloud — if you sign in with Google, your step and journey data is stored in Firebase Firestore (a Google service). Only you have access to your cloud data.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in via Google
  • Firebase Firestore — data storage
  • Firebase Crashlytics — anonymous crash reports to improve app stability
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as number of app opens and screens viewed) that help us understand how the app is used and improve it. This data is pseudonymous, is not used for advertising, and is not linked to your identity.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Deleting Your Data

You can delete all your data and your account in Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers.
